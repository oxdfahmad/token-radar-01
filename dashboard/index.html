Which protocol should you farm next?

Implied value is this radar's own estimate, not a price and not a promise. A protocol records no token in the snapshot — that is DefiLlama's record, not proof it has none.

How to read this radar

  • Watchlist is the floor, never the ceiling. The 40 watchlist protocols are the big pre-token names at $50M+ TVL. The early radar adds every other pre-token protocol the same rule keeps, so a fast-growing small protocol can outrank a large flat one.
  • Live-token protocols are set aside. They already trade, so their market cap is a fact, not a model. They appear on the Live token tab, and their market-cap / TVL ratios calibrate the multiples.
  • Wrapped, staked and bridged products are out. A wrapped or staked product of a token that already trades is not a token still to come, so the board leaves those categories and names out entirely.
  • Growth and age are the launch signals. The model weighs a week's TVL growth at 0.8 and scores age on a log scale, so a young protocol is not punished for being early and surviving longer earns a premium that fades. An unknown listed date uses the snapshot's median rankable age, never a magic number.
  • Farm plans cost real money. Every step states its capital, its gas and its time. Never pay to claim an airdrop, never approve an unlimited spend, and never farm with money you cannot lose.
